This app powers bundle pricing for The Exam Pros. When a customer adds a bundle to the cart, it shows as a single line with the bundle's name, image and price in the cart and at checkout. There's nothing to set up on this page. The app works in the background on every cart. A customer adds a bundle from the storefront. The theme marks the cart line as a bundle and attaches its name, price and image. At checkout, the app replaces the line's title, image and price with the bundle's, converted to the customer's currency. Contact your developer if bundle prices or images don't look right in checkout.
